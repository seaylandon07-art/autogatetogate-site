Abnormals

Leave the room. It keeps flying.

GateToGate is meant to assist with long flights, but it can also be used for total autonomy during flights. It is designed to keep operating no matter what happens, even if you aren’t at your desk. A failure mid-flight is handled quietly. Engine fire? No problem: GateToGate will automatically reroute to the nearest airport. If you’re present and want to take control back at any time, you can bind a hotkey in settings or click Engage again.

Setup

Plan the flight the way you already do, on the World Map or with SimBrief. GateToGate reads the route from the sim. After loading into the cockpit, find GateToGate on your toolbar and check which warnings pop up, as you may need to change a few settings. Once everything passes, press Engage.

Performance

GateToGate runs externally, outside of the sim, so it costs almost nothing in frame rate. Expect less than a 1% drop in frame rate. Engage, status and settings live in the toolbar panel inside MSFS, just like a normal mod would.
