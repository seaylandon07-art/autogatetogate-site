Autopilot for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024

It flies the whole flight.

From the departure gate to the arrival gate: pushback, taxi, takeoff, cruise, landing and shutdown.

Get GateToGate See the flight

Pushback and taxi

It gets itself to the runway.

Calls the tug, starts the engines, taxis out and talks to ATC.

Takeoff and cruise

Flown like a line pilot.

Real procedures at a real pace, with callouts and cabin announcements. Cruise at up to 16× when the sector is long.

Abnormals

Leave the room. It keeps flying.

A failure mid-flight is handled quietly. Press M any time to take the aircraft back.

Approach and landing

Down the ILS and on to the stand.

It lands, taxis in, parks and shuts the engines down.

Before you fly.

Aircraft

  • Airbus A320neo, the default MSFS 2024 aircraft
  • FlyByWire A32NX, with autoland
  • FlyByWire A380X

Setup

Plan the flight the way you already do, on the World Map or with SimBrief. GateToGate reads the route from the sim. In the cockpit, press Engage.

Performance

GateToGate runs outside the sim, so it costs almost nothing in frame rate. Engage, status and settings live in a toolbar panel inside MSFS.
