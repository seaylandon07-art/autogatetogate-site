It flies the whole flight.

GateToGate is an autopilot for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. It takes the airliner from the departure gate to the arrival gate: pushback, taxi, takeoff, cruise, landing and shutdown.

Get GateToGate

At the gate

Plan the flight the way you already do.

Pick your gate, destination and aircraft on the World Map, or load a SimBrief plan. GateToGate reads the route from the sim. There is nothing extra to set up.

When you are in the cockpit, press Engage.

Pushback and taxi

It gets itself to the runway.

GateToGate calls the tug, pushes back, starts the engines and taxis out. It talks to ATC and handles ground services along the way.

Takeoff and cruise

Flown like a line pilot would fly it.

Real procedures at a real pace, with crew callouts and cabin announcements. It rides out turbulence and windshear.

Long sector? Speed the cruise up to 16× and drop back to real time for the descent.

Abnormals

Leave the room. It keeps flying.

If something fails mid-flight, GateToGate deals with it quietly and keeps the aircraft flying. No alarms pulling you back to the desk, no handing the controls back to an empty seat.

If you are there and want the aircraft, press M and it is yours. Press Resume to give it back.

Approach and landing

Down the ILS and on to the stand.

It flies the approach, lands, taxis in, parks and shuts the engines down. The flight ends where a real one does.
